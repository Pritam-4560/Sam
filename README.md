# Samurai Sprint - Way of the Ronin

A high-octane 2D endless runner and samurai combat action game built with **Jetpack Compose**, **Kotlin**, and custom real-time canvas rendering for Android.

## Features & Mechanics

- **Fluid Acrobatic Movement**:
  - Run with dynamic parallax scrolling across feudal Japanese biomes (Sakura Forest, Torii Sunset Ridge, Stormy Castle Rooftops, and Blood Moon Netherrealm).
  - Single jump and mid-air double jump over bamboo spikes, rolling spiked barrels, and chasm pits.
  - Slide/duck under burning Torii ceiling beams and low banners.

- **Fast-Paced Katana Combat & Parrying**:
  - Razor-sharp air and ground slashes that cleave oncoming Shadow Ronin and flying Tengu crows in half.
  - **Deflection & Parry**: Strike flying ninja shurikens right before impact to reflect them back at high velocity for massive style points.
  - Style Rank multiplier ladder from Disciple (D) to Shinobi God (SSS) with dynamic combo decay timers.

- **Dragon Sprint (Bullet Time Rush)**:
  - Fill the Spirit Focus meter by slicing enemies and gathering azure spirit orbs.
  - Unleash Dragon Sprint to trigger an invincible blazing rush that pulverizes all obstacles in your path.

- **Dojo Upgrades & Armory**:
  - Unlock and switch between Shinobi Warriors:
    - **Kage** (Shadow Ronin - Balanced agility)
    - **Tomoe** (Sakura Kunoichi - Triple-jump acrobatics)
    - **Musashi** (Twin Blade Master - Extended reach & double combo multiplier)
    - **Jin** (Ghost of Tsushima - Iron fortitude with bonus health)
  - Forge powerful Katanas:
    - **Tamahagane Steel** (Reliable razor edge)
    - **Crimson Dragon** (Flaming slashes)
    - **Raikiri Thunder** (Magnetic coin pull)
    - **Sakura Void** (Rapid focus generation)
  - Level up Vitality, Slash Reach, Focus Gathering, Coin Magnetism, and Combo Flow in the Dojo.

- **Honor Trials & Quests**:
  - Complete trials for bonus coin rewards.

- **Audio & Haptics Engine**:
  - Pure Kotlin procedural waveform synthesis (slashes, metallic parry chimes, coin sparkles, jump whooshes, game over gongs).
  - Tactile haptic vibration on slashes, parries, and damage.
